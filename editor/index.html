Maps are placed where their doorways say they join, so this is the world as the player walks it. A room you enter through a door is not next to anything, so it sits in a band below with a line back to its doorway.
click a map to open it
Each gate has a colour and a number: the filled band is a mouth, the arrow runs to where it puts you down, and a dashed arrow is a one-way trip.
● where a fresh game starts
Maps are grouped by their tags, so a second zone reads as one even before anything connects it to the first.
An item is authored in content/items/. What is editable here is what the game reads off it; the sprite, the kind and the slot are structure and are shown but not changed.
price in coins - 0 means it cannot be sold
stacks and max stack travel together: a cap without stacking says nothing, and the build refuses it
equippable has to agree with the item's slot
note is optional - leave it empty and it is dropped from the file
An actor is authored in content/actors/. Its drawing, rig and wandering are structure and are not editable here.
behaviour is the whole of whether it fights: passive leaves you alone, defensive hits back once you hit it, offensive walks up and starts it
walks and walk radius travel together - a radius on something that stands still says nothing, and the build refuses it
attack is what one of its blows deals
armor comes off every blow it takes, to a floor of 1 - so armour can make a thing tough but never unkillable
hp left blank means it cannot be killed at all, which is what the sheep and the goat are
A hostile actor with no attack is refused by the build: it would chase the hero and never hurt them
A quest is authored in content/quests/. What cannot be read there is whether the world can pay it, because that is a question about every map at once - so each objective says how much of what it asks for is placed.

A conversation is a graph, so this draws it as one: a box per node, an arrow per reply, laid out in columns by how many replies deep it is.
click a node to read the whole of it
● where the conversation starts
